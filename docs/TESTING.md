# Testing

## Automated

```bash
# Watchapp: builds for aplite, basalt, chalk, diorite, emery, flint and gabbro
cd watch && pebble build

# Companion: unit tests and debug APK
cd android && ./gradlew testDebugUnitTest assembleDebug
```

flow, the gateway socket's ticket, ready frame, JSON-RPC answers, errors and events, Hermes audio download), turn outcomes over the gateway (completed, tool activity, other sessions' events ignored, new sessions, queued behind a running turn, failed, interrupted, refused resume, refused submit, unreachable, signed out, a drop before sending, and after sending: answer found in the transcript, re-attaching to the running turn, no trace, and Hermes staying offline, each without resending), recovery bound to the submitted prompt (an older identical prompt and its answer, a later turn's answer, the acked row id, and no boundary), reply audio classification (text-to-speech output only, the user's own voice note excluded, audio arriving after the text, media-only answers), reply alerts through one policy (the Ringbearer-in-Telegram acceptance case, Telegram text off with audio on, both off, late audio alerting once or refreshing silently, a failed fetch retried at the same activity, an undelivered alert rolled back and retried (including after a restart, for late audio, and from a direct completion) with bounded attempts, busy sessions, restart catch-up from saved checkpoints, the first-poll baseline, direct completions under the same policy, and a direct completion plus a later poll alerting once), dashboard URL validation (ports, schemes, hosts), credentials bound to the address that issued them, the account card's states (signed in, session token, offline, refreshed, expired, signed out), the Recent audio inbox (audio replies only, the user's voice notes excluded, or JSON booleans, malformed responses handled as protocol errors, Telegram read-only mode with other sessions writable, session filtering and flags, text fitting for the watch, protocol encoding, and that the message keys match `watch/package.json`.

## Watchapp in the emulator

`watch/tools/fake_phone.py` plays the phone side of the protocol with canned data, so the watch UI can be clicked through without Android or Hermes:

```bash
cd watch
pebble install --emulator basalt
~/.local/share/uv/tools/pebble-tool/bin/python tools/fake_phone.py basalt &
pebble emu-button click select --emulator basalt
pebble screenshot --emulator basalt
```

`fake_phone.py` takes flags after the platform: `headphones` (the phone reports headphones), `alert` (an audio reply from another session arrives 8 s after HELLO, for the banner), `textalert` (the same with a text reply opening on a short sentence and a list, for the banner's headline), `quick=N` (sends CONFIG with that Quick launch) and `approve` (a sent message makes Hermes ask before a command; the fake phone prints the answer). A SEND streams the reply in STREAM messages, then completes and resends the history with the new exchange.

The watch asks for sessions when it launches, so if the fake phone connects after the app has started, long-press Select (Refresh) to load them:

```bash
pebble emu-button push select --emulator basalt; sleep 1; pebble emu-button release select --emulator basalt
```

The Quick launch row cycles through its five choices, and each was checked by temporarily treating every launch as a Quick Launch (the emulator cannot press one): dictation opens at once and Back leaves the composer showing; *Play latest audio* opens the newest Recent audio reply and plays it; *Last session* opens it and fills in its title once the list arrives. The launcher icon was checked on basalt. On aplite, the action menu used to fault for lack of heap (also on 0.3.1); 0.4.0 lists fewer sessions there (12), shortens its outgoing buffer (no microphone, so only quick replies) and drops the session history while a reply card covers it, fetching it again on return. The home screen, conversation and banner (protocol v5) were checked this way. The banner's headline, AUDIO tag and *Select: open* hint were checked with `alert` and `textalert` on basalt, chalk and diorite: a short first sentence shows in bold with the rest below it, a long one falls back to the plain preview, and on chalk a two-line headline leaves no room for the rest, which the conversation then shows.

Approvals were checked with `approve` on basalt and chalk: the card takes the screen with Deny picked, Up moves to *Allow session* (skipping *Always allow*, which this request does not offer), Select sends `session` and the reply then streams in; Back leaves the card, the conversation tail says *Needs your OK: Select*, and Select brings the card back. Aplite has no card (it gets the phone notification): with the approval plumbing compiled out there too, Sessions → a conversation → hold Select still opens the menu, with only a few hundred bytes to spare.

The watch speaker was checked on emery the same way (`python3 tools/fake_phone.py emery`; the fake phone streams a 4 s tone for every Recent audio item except the "Repo cleanup" one, which has no audio): Play on watch speaker goes from "Buffering n%" to "Watch: Hermes audio"; Pause, Resume and Stop on watch; the reply without audio fails with "No Hermes audio for this reply"; nothing is left allocated when the app exits. The emulator has no audio output, and its speaker never takes samples, so there playback ends with "Watch speaker is not playing" after 5 s. Sound, and whether Bluetooth keeps up with 8 KB/s, can only be checked on a watch. The fake phone sends 1000-byte chunks because the emulator's serial link garbles bigger packets; the real phone sends chunks as large as the watch's inbox allows.

## Not yet tested: real devices

Nothing here has run against a real Hermes, phone or watch. Milestone 2's acceptance needs, in order:

1. **Connection.** Expose the dashboard on the tailnet (`tailscale serve --bg --https=10443 http://127.0.0.1:9119`), install the APK, enter the URL and press *Continue*: only the sign-in this dashboard offers should appear (password with browser, or session token), and *Other ways to sign in* should show all three. Sign in. Expect "Connected" with session and Telegram counts, and the sign-in form replaced by the status card with all three links green or grey. Note which sign-in provider the dashboard uses. Also try a bad port (e.g. `:91198`): the field should say the port is out of range and nothing should change. Scroll the whole screen and open the keyboard: the title bar should stay below the status bar with nothing drawn over or above it.
2. **Watch link.** Install `watch/build/watch.pbw` through the Pebble/Core app, with the debug APK installed (package `dev.wristbound.companion.debug`, listed in `companionApp`). Open Wristbound on the watch; the session list should load.
3. **Sessions and filter.** Toggle Telegram sessions; confirm the list changes and that alerts do not.
4. **History.** Open an API session and a Telegram session; compare with the desktop app.
5. **Send.** Send a harmless quick reply into an API-created session. Confirm on the desktop that it landed in that session (not a fork) and that the reply appears on the watch.
6. **Telegram-origin send.** Send a quick reply in a Telegram session. Expect working, then completed, and the turn visible in that session on the desktop (not posted into Telegram). Also send while a desktop turn is running in that session: expect "Queued behind a running turn", not an interrupted desktop turn.
7. **Watch open, background off.** With *Reply speed* set to *Only while Wristbound is open*, open the watchapp and generate a Hermes audio reply through Telegram. Within about 30 s a reply card should appear (also while that same session is on screen), with Play, Reply and Open session. Close the watchapp and confirm checking stops (the status card says replies wait until you open Wristbound).
8. **Recent audio.** Open *Recent audio* from Home: audio replies from several sessions, newest first, the new one marked `*`. Open one: Play must play that exact reply's audio, Reply must open the composer in its own session (or say read-only), Open session must show that session. Your own voice notes must not appear. Test with headphones, and with *Play on phone speaker*.
9. **Locked phone.** With *Reply speed* at *Normal* (the default), lock the phone, close the watchapp, send a message from the desktop or Telegram, and confirm one notification on the watch with Play / Play on speaker / Read on watch actions. Repeat under forced Doze (`adb shell dumpsys deviceidle force-idle`, then `unforce`): with *Allow background use* the alert should still arrive within one interval, without it within about 9 minutes. Also send a long turn from the watch, close the watchapp at once and lock the phone: the service notification should say "Waiting for Hermes to reply" and the reply should still alert, including with *Only while Wristbound is open*. Press Play on a reply over 30 s from the Pebble notification with the screen off: it should play to the end. Reboot the phone: the service comes back and checks resume.
10. **Playback.** Ask Hermes to reply with voice (its text-to-speech tool), then Play from the watch: expect "Playing Hermes audio". Open a plain text reply: its menu must have no Play rows (only Reply, Open session and Dismiss on a new-reply card; nothing in session history), and its phone notification no Play actions. With headphones: the menu shows *Play on headphones*; Pause, Resume, Replay stay on the headphones. Unplug or disconnect the headphones mid-reply: playback must pause, not move to the speaker. Without headphones: the menu shows only *Play on phone speaker* (and *Play on watch speaker* where the watch has one); connect headphones with the card open and press Select again: *Play on headphones* appears. With headphones connected, *Play on phone speaker* must come out of the phone, not the headphones.
11. **Ringbearer (acceptance).** Settings: *Messaging chats* listed, *Normal* alerts (so *Messaging text* off, *Voice replies* on), optionally *Messaging chats* set to *Read only*. Lock the phone, close the watchapp, and record on the Pebble Index so Hermes answers with automatic voice, then repeat with its text-to-speech tool. Expect exactly one notification, with a clean conversation title and Play audio action that plays the exact existing audio; the recording itself must not be offered as the reply. Nothing may play on its own. Repeat with a reply whose audio arrives after the text (one alert, when the audio arrives), with a text-only reply (no alert), with airplane mode toggled across the reply (still one alert), and after killing and reopening the companion (one alert, no repeats). With messaging chats read only, the session's Reply row must say "Read-only here" and refuse to open, while other sessions still send. Use the matching Hermes attachment backend for automatic voice; see [HERMES_API.md](HERMES_API.md).
12. **Watch speaker** (Pebble Time 2 or Pebble 2 Duo). Open a Recent audio item, then *Play on watch speaker*. Expect "Preparing audio", "Buffering n%", then "Watch: Hermes audio" and the reply audible from the watch, with nothing from the phone. Check Pause/Resume, Stop, and Replay on watch (Replay should start quickly: the phone keeps the clip). Try a reply over 2 minutes (the label ends "first 2 min"), a text-only reply (no *Play on watch speaker* row), the watch on mute ("Watch speaker is muted"), walking out of Bluetooth range mid-clip ("Phone disconnected" within about 10 s, never sound from the phone), and leaving the watchapp mid-clip (playback stops). Note if playback stutters back to "Buffering": that means the link is slower than 8 KB/s on that watch.
13. **Quick Launch.** In the Pebble app's settings, assign Wristbound to a Quick Launch button. In the companion app, *On the watch → Opens on*: try each choice by long-pressing that button from the watchface. *Dictate new session* should be listening within a second.
14. **Which sessions alert.** With the defaults, a cron job that answers in text must not alert, and one that answers with Hermes audio must alert once; a desktop chat reply alerts; Hermes following up on a background process in a desktop chat does not. Scheduled sessions stay off the watch's list until *Show scheduled and automation* is on, and a pinned session is listed first. Each of these should appear under *Recent alerts* on the companion's home screen, labelled BUZZED or SILENT with the switch that decided it; with *Hide reply text* on, the list shows no reply text. Switching the preset (Quiet, Normal, All) should change the ■/□ list under it, and flipping one switch under *Customise* should show *Custom*.
15. **Approvals.** From the watch, ask Hermes to do something that needs approval (for example to delete a scratch folder with a shell command). Expect the card at once with a long vibration, Deny picked. Deny: Hermes reports it was refused. Again with *Allow once*: it runs. Again with the watchapp closed: a phone notification with the answers as actions, answerable from the Pebble. Answer one on the desktop instead: the watch card goes away.
16. **Status card.** Block Wristbound's notifications in Android settings: the card turns amber with *Notifications are blocked* and *Allow notifications*. Restore them, then set battery to optimised with *Reply speed* on *Normal*: *Android may hold replies back* with *Allow background use*. Turn Wi-Fi and data off: *Cannot reach Hermes* with *Check again*.
17. **Reliability.** Kill the companion during a turn and reopen; toggle airplane mode mid-turn (expect the reply once the phone reconnects, or *uncertain*, never a duplicate turn); restart Hermes mid-turn (expect *uncertain*). Leave the phone signed in for more than a day to confirm token refresh.

Record the watch model and firmware, phone model, Android version, Pebble app version and Hermes version with each run.
