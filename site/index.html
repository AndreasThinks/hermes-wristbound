Each works alone. Together, Hermes talks back.

Pebble + Android

Watch app

The full Hermes client on the wrist, with its Android companion. Browse sessions, read conversations as they stream in, reply by dictation or a quick answer, approve steps, and play Hermes' audio on headphones, the phone or the watch speaker.

Pebble app · Android and iOS

Index plugin

Makes Hermes an agent for the Index ring and any plugin-aware app. Say Tell Hermes … and the message goes straight to your Hermes: no Telegram, no watch app needed. Tick Reply with voice to have it answered out loud.

Pebble Store

Your Hermes host

Voice plugin

A small Hermes plugin, wristbound-voice, that speaks the reply to what you just dictated with your own Hermes text-to-speech, the way /voice does, and keeps the clip so the watch can play it. No modified Hermes.

How it fits

Say it to Index. Feel it on your wrist.

  1. 01IndexYou say Tell Hermes to check the train times.
  2. 02Index pluginSends it to your Hermes dashboard over your tailnet.
  3. 03Hermes + voice pluginHermes answers; the plugin speaks the answer.
  4. 04CompanionHears the change live and fetches the reply and its clip.
  5. 05WatchBuzzes once, dot dot dash, with Play.

Hermes stays in charge of every session, turn and audio file. Wristbound keeps no conversation store of its own, and the watch never holds a password. Telegram, desktop and CLI sessions show up too, and you can continue them from the watch.

Status

Experimental, and honest about it.

  • Session browsing, replies and headphone playback have been used on a Pixel 9a, a Pebble Time 2 and Hermes 0.22.2. The Index plugin has been used on a Pixel with Index.
  • Newer paths, such as live updates, voice replies and watch-speaker playback, pass automated and emulator tests and still need checks on real hardware.
  • The Android companion is a sideloaded APK. There is no iOS companion yet; the Index plugin is built for iOS but untested there.

Hermes Wristbound is an independent project for Hermes Agent and Pebble, not affiliated with either.
